Amino Acids

Our amino acid portfolio covers the full spectrum of essential amino acids (EAAs), branched-chain amino acids (BCAAs), and conditionally essential amino acids. Sourced from premium manufacturers, each batch is tested for purity, potency, and heavy-metal compliance. Ideal for sports nutrition, recovery drinks, and functional food fortification.

  • ✓ L-Leucine, L-Isoleucine, L-Valine
  • ✓ L-Glutamine, L-Arginine, L-Citrulline
  • ✓ Custom blend formulations available

Minerals

Our mineral ingredients are selected for optimal bioavailability and stability. We offer both inorganic and chelated mineral forms to suit different formulation requirements. Each lot is analyzed for purity, particle size, and heavy-metal content to ensure safety and efficacy across all applications.

  • ✓ Calcium, Magnesium, Zinc, Iron
  • ✓ Chelated mineral complexes
  • ✓ Trace minerals & specialty blends

Herbal Extracts

Our standardized herbal extracts are produced using advanced extraction technologies to preserve bioactive compounds. We partner with trusted growers worldwide to source botanicals with full traceability. Each extract is tested for marker compounds, contaminants, and microbiological safety.

  • ✓ Adaptogens & nootropics
  • ✓ Traditional herbal remedies
  • ✓ Standardized marker compounds
